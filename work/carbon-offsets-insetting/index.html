Carbon Offsets Are Dead. Long Live Carbon Insetting.

Why carbon offsets have lost credibility—and why investing inside a company’s own value chain may be more accountable.

  • By Paul Shannon
  • Original publication LinkedIn
  • Date May 15, 2025
  • Reading time 2 min
View original on LinkedIn ↗

About this work

This article compares carbon offsetting with carbon insetting, an approach that directs climate investment into a company’s own operations or value chain. The piece focuses on credibility, accountability, boundaries, and the practical difference between purchasing a claim and changing the system that produces emissions.

Explaining a contested idea

Offsets and insets are not interchangeable, and neither label guarantees impact. Paul defines the distinction before evaluating why insetting can provide a clearer relationship between spending, suppliers, operational change, and reported outcomes.

Balanced sustainability writing

The article avoids treating a new term as an automatic solution. It frames insetting as a potentially more accountable path while keeping measurement quality, additionality, implementation, and corporate incentives in view.

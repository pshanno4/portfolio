Project in 60 seconds

What it covers—and what Paul contributed.

This Workinman Interactive release article turns a long feature list into a clear player story. The update combined faster performance, reduced load times, new questions, online one-versus-one matches, QR and code-based joining, stronger MotionPicks comparisons, and a continuing stream of movie content.

The assignment required product accuracy without reading like release notes. Paul organized the copy around what players could do faster, what they could do together, and why the app would remain useful after the first session.

Editorial approach

The article leads with the overall experience—faster, smoother, and more social—before explaining the mechanics.

Studio collaboration

What the piece demonstrates

Product-update writing that prioritizes audience benefit

Editorial approach

The article leads with the overall experience—faster, smoother, and more social—before explaining the mechanics. The 1v1 section translates the match flow into plain language, while later passages distinguish Name That Movie, Shuffle, and MotionPicks for readers who have not used the app.

Studio collaboration

Paul worked within a multidisciplinary studio process. Product stakeholders supplied the update priorities; editors and executives shaped positioning and scope; artists and developers provided the visual and technical context needed to describe the experience accurately. The final copy keeps those inputs coherent without exposing the internal brief to the reader.

What the piece demonstrates

  • Product-update writing that prioritizes audience benefit
  • Translation of interface and multiplayer features into concise consumer language
  • Tone control across entertainment, technology, and client positioning

Paul’s role

Paul translated the approved product brief and studio feedback into the published update article for Workinman Interactive.

What it covers—and what Paul contributed.

Artificial intelligence has environmental costs, but it is also changing how organizations measure, forecast, and manage sustainability work. This article examines that tension for business readers rather than presenting AI as either an uncomplicated climate solution or a single source of harm.

Holding two claims at once

Useful analysis has to account for energy, water, and infrastructure demands while also examining applications in modeling, monitoring, reporting, and operational decision-making.

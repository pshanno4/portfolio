The hidden middle of client work

Online advice often treats acquisition as the whole business. Paul focuses instead on the operating period in which expectations are managed, value is delivered, invoices are collected, and the next assignment is either earned or lost.

Professional perspective

The piece uses direct commentary to make business mechanics approachable to newer freelancers. Its purpose is practical: help readers identify why a busy calendar can still produce unstable income.

About this work

Issue #5 of Sustainable585 collects environmental research, local projects, and the quieter institutional work shaping sustainability across greater Rochester. The recurring format helps regional readers see connections among developments that would otherwise appear as isolated updates.

Regional curation with context

The assignment was not simply to collect links. Paul selected developments, checked their local relevance, and supplied the connective explanation needed to show how research, infrastructure, community decisions, and long-term systems work intersect.

A repeatable editorial format

This issue continues a compact dispatch model: identify timely local work, explain why it matters, and give readers enough context to follow the subject beyond a single news cycle.

Paul’s role

Paul independently researched, curated, wrote, and published the Sustainable585 dispatch.

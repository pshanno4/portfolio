Think Your Water Is Clean? Climate Change Might Be Proving You Wrong

What harmful algal blooms can look like, why climate conditions matter, and why apparently clear water is not the whole story.

About this work

Water can look ordinary while environmental conditions are shifting beneath the surface. This public-facing explainer introduces harmful algal blooms, recognizable warning signs, cyanobacterial risk, and the ways warming water and nutrient loading can make blooms more likely.

Writing for public safety without alarmism

The central editorial task was to make risk legible without implying that every discolored lake is identical or that appearance alone can confirm toxicity. The article uses visible bloom descriptions as an entry point, then moves toward the environmental mechanisms that require monitoring and testing.

Relevant expertise

The piece draws on Paul’s continuing work with freshwater systems and harmful algal blooms. That subject familiarity supports more precise explanations of eutrophication, temperature, runoff, and the difference between an observed condition and a supported causal claim.

Paul’s role

Paul researched and wrote the environmental-health explainer independently.

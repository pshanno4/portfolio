Project in 60 seconds

What it covers—and what Paul contributed.

Water can look ordinary while environmental conditions are shifting beneath the surface. This public-facing explainer introduces harmful algal blooms, recognizable warning signs, cyanobacterial risk, and the ways warming water and nutrient loading can make blooms more likely.

Writing for public safety without alarmism

The central editorial task was to make risk legible without implying that every discolored lake is identical or that appearance alone can confirm toxicity.

Relevant expertise

The piece draws on Paul’s continuing work with freshwater systems and harmful algal blooms.

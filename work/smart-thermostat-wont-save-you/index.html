Project in 60 seconds

What it covers—and what Paul contributed.

A smart thermostat can reduce energy use; it cannot substitute for the policy, grid, building, and infrastructure changes required at climate scale. This article uses one familiar device to explain the difference between an individual efficiency measure and a systemic response.

A concrete entry point

The thermostat makes an abstract debate visible.

Avoiding a false choice

The piece does not argue that household action is worthless.

A concrete entry point

The thermostat makes an abstract debate visible. Paul begins with a product many readers understand, acknowledges its legitimate benefit, and then widens the frame to the systems that determine the scale and durability of emissions reductions.

Avoiding a false choice

The piece does not argue that household action is worthless. It argues that personal action and structural change operate at different levels, and that communication becomes misleading when one is used to excuse the absence of the other.

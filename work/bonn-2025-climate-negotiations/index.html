What it covers—and what Paul contributed.

The Bonn climate meetings receive far less public attention than the annual COP summits, even though they shape technical negotiations, implementation work, and the decisions later presented on the larger stage. This article introduces that less visible process for readers who follow climate news but do not work inside international policy.

Why the angle matters

A meeting can be consequential without producing the dramatic imagery or final declarations that drive news coverage.

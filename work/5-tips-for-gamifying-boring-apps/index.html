5 Tips for Gamifying Even the Most Boring App

How progression, habit loops, achievements, competition, and surprise can make utility apps more engaging.

  • By Paul Shannon
  • Original publication Workinman Interactive
  • Date 2025
  • Reading time 2 min
View original on Workinman Interactive ↗

About this work

This Workinman article translates gamification from a broad promise into five systems product teams can actually discuss: progression, daily habit loops, achievements, social competition, and moments of surprise. The examples move across corporate tools, commerce, health, education, and service applications.

Turning mechanics into product guidance

The piece explains what each mechanic does, where it can fail, and how it connects to retention or feature discovery. Paul kept the recommendations specific—such as visible progress, meaningful thresholds, guided habits, time-bounded competition, and feedback—without pretending that adding points automatically improves an app.

Audience and positioning

The audience includes product owners and organizations that may not think of themselves as game companies. The article makes game-design knowledge accessible while showing how Workinman can connect strategy, interface, analytics, and implementation.

Studio collaboration

The concept, examples, tone, and scope were refined through Workinman’s studio workflow with editors, executives, artists, and other contributors. Paul developed the approved direction into a structured, search-friendly article rather than a generic list of engagement tips.

Paul’s role

Paul wrote and structured the article from Workinman’s editorial direction and gamification expertise.

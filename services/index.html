How projects start

One defined first assignment.

Projects can include web copy, interview-led thought leadership, source review, substantive editing, fact-checking support and overflow work within your editorial process.

  1. Share the goal.Send the subject or URL, intended reader, available material and timing.
  2. Agree the scope.We define deliverables, price, reviewers and revisions before work begins.
  3. Build from the evidence.Research, source review and a clear structure guide the work.
  4. Review and hand off.Writing, diagnosis and implementation can be scoped separately.

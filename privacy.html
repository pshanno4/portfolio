Site information

Privacy

PaulWrites collects only the information you choose to send through the project inquiry form or by email.

Project inquiries

The inquiry form may collect your name, email address, organization, website, project type, audience, timeline, source-material status, and the project details you provide. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential work.

Form processing

The form is processed by Formspree. Formspree may process submission data and technical information needed to deliver the message and limit spam. Do not send confidential, regulated, or highly sensitive information through the form.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not currently use an analytics service, advertising tracker, user account, or marketing cookie. External sites linked from the portfolio apply their own privacy practices.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask about information you submitted or request its deletion, email pshannon@paulwrites.net.
